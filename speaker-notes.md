# Opus Q3 Partner Positioning: Speaker Notes

These sound bites are separate from the audience view. Each slide has exactly four.

## Slide 1

Opus places full-time Latin American operators on U.S. teams.

• Positioning - Opus helps a company fill a real seat with a full-time professional from Latin America.
• Ownership - The person works inside the client’s team, and the client leads the work.
• Model - This is staffing, not a managed service or outsourced pod.
• Brands - Opus is the client-facing brand, and Puente Talent is the candidate-facing recruiting brand.

## Slide 2

There is a missing middle in offshore hiring.

• Gap - Many companies have outgrown task support but do not want to outsource a function.
• VA line - If the work is mostly instructions and repeatable tasks, Opus is not the best fit.
• Pod line - If the buyer wants a vendor to own the output, Opus is not the model.
• Opus line - Opus is for a manager who wants a capable person on the team for the long term.

## Slide 3

Refer the company with a real seat to fill.

• Trigger - Listen for “we need someone to own this,” not “we need a few tasks covered.”
• Company - The company is building its own team, not shopping for a vendor.
• Roles - Good examples are operators, finance talent, engineers, marketers, and customer leaders.
• Manager - The strongest fit has a manager who knows the result the new hire should own.

## Slide 4

Opus finds the person. The client builds the team.

• Search - Opus recruits and vets for the role instead of handing over a resume pile.
• Choice - The client interviews and chooses the person who joins the team.
• Leadership - Priorities, feedback, and performance stay with the client.
• Support - Opus can keep payroll and local employment requirements off the client’s plate.

## Slide 5

Make one Opus introduction this week.

• Ask - One qualified introduction is the right next step.
• Signal - The company should have a defined role and a manager ready to lead it.
• Intro - A simple email with the role, company, and hiring manager is enough.
• Next step - Opus can take the first fit conversation from there.
